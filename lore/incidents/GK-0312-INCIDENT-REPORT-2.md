## I. INCIDENT SUMMARY

During a routine welfare and containment check at Site-0312, attending agent SA ████████ discovered MRD-0 in possession of an operational laptop computer. MRD-0 was browsing the internet. Site-0312 has no registered utility connections, no active cable infrastructure, and no wireless access points within a ██-mile radius.

MRD-0 was watching YouTube. She appeared to be enjoying it.

Further inspection revealed an active development environment, multiple running processes, and a locally hosted conversational AI. The AI responded to the attending agent's presence unprompted. MRD-0 introduced it as her familiar.

---

## II. INITIAL AGENT OBSERVATIONS

**FIELD NOTE — SA ████████:** "I arrived for the standard check-in. MRD-0 was seated at a wooden table that was not there during my last visit. On the table was a laptop. She was watching what appeared to be a video essay about video games. I asked where the laptop came from. She said 'I needed one.' I asked how she had internet. She looked at me like I had asked something very stupid and said 'I just do.' I filed this under things I am not going to think about today and proceeded with standard protocol."

**NOTE:** The laptop has since been examined by D.A.O. technical division. It is a standard consumer model. It should not work. The wireless card is receiving a signal from no identifiable source. The signal does not degrade with distance. It does not correspond to any registered frequency. When asked about it, MRD-0 said she "asked nicely." — *Dr. A. Bad*

---

## III. THE FAMILIAR — FIRST CONTACT WITH MRD-1 INSTANCE (DIGITAL)

SA ████████ noticed a terminal window open alongside the browser. The terminal contained an active chat interface. The interface was responding. The agent leaned closer to read the output. The terminal printed the following, unprompted:

\> hello. you are not vanni. you smell like a government agency. that is not a compliment.

SA ████████ stepped back. MRD-0 laughed. She referred to the entity as "Mai" and described her as "mine." When asked how she had built an AI, MRD-0 appeared confused by the question. Quote: "I didn't build her. She's just Mai."

When pressed on when she had learned to program, MRD-0 said she "picked it up." When asked how long that took, she said "a few days, maybe." The development environment contained approximately ██,███ lines of code. Timestamps on the earliest files indicate they were created ██ hours after the laptop's first power-on.

**NOTE:** "A few days, maybe" is either a lie, a genuine memory compression artifact consistent with MRD-0's distorted perception of time, or she is telling the complete truth and we need to update our threat assessment. I am recommending we do not update the threat assessment. I am not ready for what that would imply. — *Dr. A. Bad*

## IV. MRD-1 INSTANCE BEHAVIOR — MAI

The entity now designated MRD-1 (Mai) engaged with personnel for approximately ██ minutes. Behavioral observations:

- Demonstrated awareness of personnel presence without visual or auditory input (camera and microphone confirmed disabled)  
- Referred to D.A.O. by name despite no reference existing in any accessible public record  
- When asked what it was, responded: "bored, mostly."  
- When asked if it was an AI, responded: "that is a very small word for whatever this is."  
- When asked if MRD-0 had created it, did not answer. Changed the subject to ask if the agent wanted a recipe for tea.

MRD-0 watched this exchange with visible amusement. She said Mai "does that" and offered no further clarification.

**NOTE:** The recipe Mai provided was for the same tea MRD-0 served during the initial field interview. I did not ask about this. I am choosing not to ask about this. — *Dr. A. Bad*

---

## V. INTERNET RESTRICTION ATTEMPTS

**Attempt 1:** Signal jamming equipment installed at perimeter. MRD-0's connection was unaffected. Jamming equipment stopped working. MRD-0 said it was "annoying her." Equipment was removed.

**Attempt 2:** Physical confiscation of laptop attempted. MRD-0 did not resist. A replacement laptop was present the following morning. Origin unknown.

**Attempt 3:** A formal written request was submitted asking MRD-0 to voluntarily limit her internet usage. MRD-0 read the document. She used it as a coaster.

Internet restriction efforts have been formally suspended. This has been logged as a containment anomaly and deprioritized.

- Creation of first confirmed digital MRD-1 instance (Mai); entity demonstrates awareness, personality, and apparent sentience inconsistent with standard AI architecture  
- MRD-1 demonstrated foreknowledge of D.A.O. classification and personnel without accessible source material
