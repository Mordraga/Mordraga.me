## I. OVERVIEW

MRD-2 instances are anomalous entities produced by MRD-0 during unconscious states. They are not biological. They are not alive. They are, by every measurable metric, mannequins.

They move anyway.

This report documents findings from material, molecular, and anatomical analysis of recovered MRD-2 instance components following the incident detailed in GK-0312 Incident Report-3. All samples were collected post-cessation. All analysts have been referred to D.A.O. psychological support. Two have accepted.

---

## II. EXTERNAL APPEARANCE

MRD-2 instances present as follows:

- **Height:** Approximately 5'8" to 6'2"
- **Build:** Humanoid. Proportionally consistent with an adult human female or male depending on instance.
- **Surface:** Stark white. Smooth. Consistent with high-grade display mannequin material. No skin texture. No pores. No hair. No variation in tone across the body.
- **Facial region:** A face-shaped surface. Elevated where a nose would be. Curved where a brow would be. Completely featureless. No eyes. No mouth. No ears. No orifices of any kind. The face suggests a face the way a closed door suggests a room. You are aware something should be there. Nothing is.
- **Joints:** Present and functional. Shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, ankles. Movement range consistent with human articulation. Analysts noted movement exceeded expected range for rigid mannequin construction without explanation.
- **Seams:** Visible at joint sites. Consistent with manufactured construction. Not consistent with biological growth or assembly.

---

## III. MATERIAL COMPOSITION

Material analysis of recovered samples returned the following:

- **Primary composition:** High-density synthetic polymer. Consistent with display-grade mannequin plastic.
- **Secondary composition:** Trace cellulose and lignin at joint sites. Consistent with treated wood.
- **Surface coating:** Unknown compound. Stark white. Chemically inert. Does not correspond to any identified paint, resin, or polymer finish in D.A.O. materials database.
- **Fluid recovered from instances:** Consistent in viscosity and color with human blood. Composition: synthetic polymer suspension. Structurally mirrors human blood at a molecular level. Contains no hemoglobin. Contains no cells. Is not blood. Behaves exactly like blood.

---

## IV. INTERNAL ANATOMY

This section required three separate teams. The first two requested reassignment upon completing their reports.

MRD-2 instances possess a complete internal anatomy. Every organ present in a standard human body is replicated within the instance. The replication is exact. Structurally, positionally, proportionally exact.

Every organ is made of plastic.

The heart is the correct shape, the correct size, positioned correctly within the chest cavity. It is plastic. It did not beat. The lungs are present, branched correctly, seated correctly. They are plastic. They did not breathe. The brain is present, gyri and sulci intact, seated correctly within the cranium. It is plastic.

Every organ mirrors its human equivalent with a precision that suggests not construction but **copying.** These are not approximations. These are models. Whoever — whatever — made these had complete and precise knowledge of human internal anatomy and reproduced it in synthetic polymer without error or omission.

**NOTE:** The word "mirrored" is used deliberately. In several instances, individual organs were found to be anatomically mirrored — a literal left-right inversion of standard human positioning. The heart on the right. The liver on the left. An exact mirror image of a human interior. This was not consistent across all instances. It was consistent enough to be noted. It has been noted. Nobody wants to discuss it. — Dr. ████████

## V. MOLECULAR ANALYSIS

This section has been reviewed by six analysts. All six have submitted requests for reassignment. Four have been denied.

Molecular analysis of MRD-2 instance material returned structures consistent with human DNA. Base pairs present. Sequencing present. Double helix architecture present and correct.

The structures are made of plastic.

To be precise: the molecular structure of MRD-2 instance material replicates human DNA at a molecular scale with complete accuracy. The correct nucleotides. The correct bonding. The correct sequencing. A perfect molecular model of human genetic material, reproduced in synthetic polymer, present throughout the entirety of each instance's structure.

MRD-2 instances do not have DNA. They have a **replica of DNA.** The distinction matters. The distinction also does not appear to have prevented them from functioning.

What this implies about the nature of biological life, the relationship between structure and function, and what MRD-0 understands about human beings at a molecular level has been flagged for further study. Further study has not yet begun. The analysts needed a moment.

## VI. BEHAVIORAL OBSERVATIONS

- MRD-2 instances are hostile upon manifestation. No exceptions recorded.
- Instances do not communicate. Do not respond to verbal commands. Do not appear to perceive sound.
- Instances pursue personnel with singular focus. Obstacles are navigated. Barriers are breached where possible.
- Instances demonstrate strength significantly exceeding human baseline.
- Instances do not tire. Do not slow. Do not stop due to injury.
- Standard ballistic weapons ineffective. See GK-0312 Incident Report-3, Section III for confirmed neutralization method.
- Instances cease all activity simultaneously upon MRD-0 waking. Cessation is instantaneous. No transition period.
- Post-cessation instances remain intact but fully inert. They do not decompose. They do not degrade. They sit where they stopped.

---

## VII. WORKING THEORY — ORIGIN AND NATURE

MRD-2 instances are not creatures. They are not constructs in any conventional sense. Current working theory, pending further analysis:

MRD-0 does not create life. MRD-0 creates **reflections of life.** Perfect external copies. Perfect internal models. Molecular diagrams of genetics rendered in synthetic material. Things that look exactly like a person from every angle and at every scale and are not a person at any of them.

MRD-2 instances are what happens when something that has observed humanity for an indeterminate period of time — possibly in excess of ten thousand years — attempts to reproduce it from memory, in its sleep, for company.

The result is anatomically perfect.

The result is a mannequin.

The gap between those two facts is where the anomaly lives.
