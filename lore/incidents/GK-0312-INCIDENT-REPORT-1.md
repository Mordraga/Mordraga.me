## I. INCIDENT SUMMARY

At approximately ██:██ PM local time, ██████████ Police Department received a report from the parents of two minors claiming a woman had threatened their children at Site-0312, the condemned ██████████ Cemetery. Officers responded and, finding the subject uncooperative with removal requests, escalated to physical escort. The subject could not be moved. SWAT was contacted. The FBI was contacted. F.E.T.B. Taskforce Delta-7 assumed jurisdiction. Total escalation window: approximately 4 hours.

The subject, now designated MRD-0, was cooperative throughout. She appeared confused about why anyone was there.

---

## II. PRE-ARRIVAL INCIDENT — CIVILIAN WITNESS ACCOUNTS

**WITNESS STATEMENT — MINOR A (age ██):** "We were just messing around in there. She came out of nowhere. She was just standing there and then she wasn't normal anymore. Her mouth opened wrong. Like all the way wrong. There were too many teeth. I don't know how to explain it. We ran. I don't want to go back there."

**WITNESS STATEMENT — MINOR B (age ██):** "I didn't look. \[MINOR A\] looked. I just ran."

**NOTE:** When MRD-0 was asked about this incident during the field interview, she had no recollection of the minors being on the property. She stated: "I would have remembered chasing off trespassers. That's the job." She did not appear to be lying. This is consistent with MRD-0's apparent memory gaps surrounding anomalous behavior. — *Dr. A. Bad*

## III. POLICE AND SWAT INCIDENT LOG

- **Responding officers:** 3 (██████████ PD)  
- **SWAT personnel:** 6  
- **Injuries sustained:** 0 (physical). 2 officers requested leave following incident.  
- **Equipment damage:** 1 standard-issue patrol vehicle side mirror.

Officers found MRD-0 tending to a grave marker in the eastern section of the cemetery. She was described as "completely calm," "unbothered," and "a little annoyed, actually." Officers requested the subject leave the property. She declined, citing that she was "on the clock." Three officers attempted physical escort. All three were unable to move the subject past the property boundary. Officers described the experience as "pushing a building." One officer stated verbatim: "She wasn't resisting. She was just standing there. She didn't even put her coffee down."

SWAT arrived and attempted the same. Six personnel with full tactical equipment were unable to move MRD-0 more than approximately 0.3 meters before she stepped back without apparent effort. MRD-0 told them to "stop it, you're scuffing my boots." A SWAT vehicle was repositioned to block the property entrance. MRD-0 walked around it, returned to the grave marker, and resumed work. The side mirror made contact with the gate in the process. It is unclear how.

**NOTE:** Weight measurements were not taken during the incident. Subsequent estimation based on ground impression data suggests MRD-0 displaces approximately 1.8–2.2 metric tons during anomalous states. This does not appear to be visible to the naked eye. Officers described her as "normal sized." She is not. — *Dr. A. Bad*

## IV. F.E.T.B. ARRIVAL AND CONTAINMENT ASSESSMENT

Taskforce Delta-7 arrived and immediately suspended physical removal attempts. Site-0312 was designated a de facto containment site. MRD-0 showed no aggression and had not left the property boundary under her own power at any point during the four-hour escalation window. It was determined that the property boundary itself may constitute a passive containment mechanism.

MRD-0 confirmed this, stating she was "the caretaker of the crypt" and couldn't leave while "on shift." Attempts to clarify what shift means for a person employed by owners who died in 1918 were not productive. Acquisition of the property by D.A.O. was initiated within 48 hours. MRD-0's primary concern was rent stability.
