## I. INCIDENT SUMMARY

At approximately ██:██ AM, Site-0312 night personnel reported the spontaneous appearance of multiple unidentified biological entities on the property grounds. Entities were bipedal, humanoid in approximate shape, and possessed no facial features of any kind. Entities immediately engaged site personnel in a hostile manner.

Two fatalities were confirmed. ██ additional personnel sustained injuries ranging from minor lacerations to significant trauma.

All entity activity ceased at approximately ██:██ AM, coinciding precisely with MRD-0 waking naturally from sleep in the on-site residential structure. MRD-0 was unaware any incident had occurred. On a lighter note that morning, MRD-0 made bacon and eggs and offered some to the surviving staff alongside tea. After the tea was scanned for anomalous properties it was revealed to be normal ginseng tea.

---

## II. ENTITY DESCRIPTION AND BEHAVIOR

Entities, hereafter designated MRD-2 instances (Threat Level 2 — Hostile), were described consistently across all surviving witness accounts as follows: humanoid in structure, approximately 5'8" to 6'2" in height. Entities presented with a distinctly wooden appearance — jointed limbs, segmented torso, and a smooth brown texture inconsistent with human skin. The head was shaped to suggest a face but possessed no features whatsoever. No eyes, no mouth, no ears. A face-shaped absence. Movement was described as "wrong" and "too smooth" by multiple witnesses, inconsistent with the rigid jointed structure of the body. Entities did not vocalize. They did not hesitate. They moved with singular apparent purpose: locating and engaging personnel. For full physical and material analysis see: MRD-2 Analysis Report.

Biological testing on recovered tissue samples returned INCONCLUSIVE. Material composition analysis identified entities as being constructed primarily of cellulose, lignin, and synthetic polymer consistent with wood and plastic. No organic tissue was identified. Molecular analysis of recovered samples revealed structures consistent with human DNA at a molecular level — base pairs, sequencing, architecture all present and correct. The structures are made of plastic. They have never been alive. Results were reviewed three times. They did not change. See: MRD-2 Analysis Report.

## III. USE OF FORCE — WEAPONS EFFICACY LOG

**Standard issue firearms: INEFFECTIVE.**

Multiple rounds were discharged into MRD-2 instances at center mass. Entities bled. Entities did not stop. One instance sustained what would constitute a fatal wound in any standard biological organism and continued engaging personnel for a further ██ minutes without any apparent reduction in function. Entities do not appear to require organs to operate. They bleed because they are biological. They continue because they are not alive in any sense that stopping bleeding would address.

**Effective neutralization method: LIMB REMOVAL.**

Discovered at approximately ██:██ AM by Guard ████████, who is commended for their composure under extreme circumstances and their applied knowledge of unconventional threat neutralization. Removing limbs rendered MRD-2 instances immobile. Immobilized instances remained biological but ceased pursuit.

Guard ████████ has been awarded a ████████ commendation. Guard ████████ has also requested indefinite reassignment. This has been approved without question.

## IV. CASUALTIES AND INJURIES

- **Fatalities:** 2\. Both deaths resulted from extreme physical trauma consistent with being dismembered by an entity of significant strength. Nature of injuries has been classified at SIGMA-4. Families of deceased have been notified via standard D.A.O. cover protocol.  
- **Additional injuries:** ██ personnel. Lacerations, fractures, and blunt trauma.  
- All surviving personnel have been referred to D.A.O. psychological support.  
- Three personnel have resigned. This is being processed without comment.

## V. INCIDENT TERMINATION

At ██:██ AM all MRD-2 instances ceased activity simultaneously. Mobile instances stopped moving. Immobilized instances became fully inert. No transition period. No gradual cessation. One moment they were active. The next they were not.

Tissue samples collected post-cessation confirm entities remain biological but show no further anomalous behavior. They are, at time of writing, simply flesh. Sitting where they stopped.

Timestamp of cessation has been cross-referenced with on-site residential structure logs. It corresponds exactly with MRD-0's alarm activating. She had set it for ██:██ AM. She woke up on time. On a lighter note that morning, MRD-0 made bacon and eggs and offered some to the surviving staff alongside tea. After the tea was scanned for anomalous properties it was revealed to be normal ginseng tea. She did not know.

**JA ████████:** "Good morning. How are you feeling today?"

And then… I don't know. People were getting hurt. It was really bad. I don't usually have dreams like that."

**JA ████████:** *\[no response recorded. 11 second pause.\]*

**JA ████████:** "That does sound upsetting. Do you know what might have caused it?"

**JA ████████:** *\[no response recorded. 6 second pause.\]* "Yes. Very weird."

**MRD-0:** "Are you okay? You look a little pale."

**JA ████████:** "I'm fine. Long night. Would you like some tea?"

*Interview terminated at this point. Full transcript ends.*

- MRD-0 is capable of manifesting biological MRD-2 instances during unconscious states without apparent intent or awareness  
- Instances are fully biological, hostile when dream state is hostile, and cease upon MRD-0 waking  
- Standard ballistic weapons ineffective. Limb removal confirmed as neutralization method  
- MRD-0 experienced the incident as a nightmare. She described it accurately. She does not know it was real  
- MRD-0's emotional state (loneliness) appears to be a direct trigger for manifestation. This has been flagged for immediate follow-up. **If MRD-0 remains lonely, this will happen again.**
