⸸ GK-0312-TL1-MRD-0 — Entity Summary
DESIGNATION: MRD-0 KNOWN ALIASES: Mordraga, Mordraga0, Vanni, Vanessa, ██████, ████████████, and ████ CLASSIFICATION: Persistent liminal entity THREAT LEVEL: TL1 — Cooperative / Low immediate risk LOCATION: Site-0312 · ███████, USA · The Crypt PRONOUNS: She / They (field use: It, per ████████ family, who hold the property deed) CONTAINMENT: Self-contained. Subject cannot leave the property boundary. Further containment deemed unnecessary.

MRD-0 came to F.E.T.B. attention via local law enforcement report: a woman identified as "Vanessa" was present at a condemned cemetery and refused — or was unable — to leave when asked. Officers reported being unable to physically move subject past the property line despite sustained effort. The F.B.I. escalated. F.E.T.B. Taskforce Delta-7 "Void Watchers" responded.

Subject believes she is employed by the property's last recorded owners, who died in 1918. Subject believes she has lived at the site for approximately five years. Subject believes she is twenty-six years old.

Agents assigned to Site-0312 have since confirmed that MRD-0's subjective experience of time bears no reliable relationship to measurable time. She is not lying. She does not know.

✦ Physical Description — Documented
HEIGHT: 5'11" (documented) · Tall enough to be threatening in a hallway. WEIGHT (DOC): 200 lb (reported by subject) WEIGHT (ACT): Approximately 2 tons. Mechanism unknown. Subject does not appear to notice. APPEARANCE: Adult female, caucasian, early adulthood. Ghostly pale skin. Angular oval face. Defining features: mouth, canines, septum piercing, earrings. HAIR: Long black, typically tied back. Always appears to have just exited a fight or a summoning. Red dyed streaks — possibly dyed. Classification: possibly not dyed. EYES: Pinkish-Brown at baseline. See: Eye Color Scale. MARKINGS: Rose tattoo across the clavicle. Significance being investigated. DRESS: Red or black, always. Jewelry with pagan iconography. Accessories vary — see Objects of Interest.

⸸ Anomalous Properties — Confirmed
  • ▸ Liminal Binding. MRD-0 cannot leave the property boundary of Site-0312. She walks to the line and stops. She does not know why. Agents who have asked are told she is "at work." The boundary holds regardless of physical intervention, persuasion, or instructions from the Bureau.
  • ▸ Mass Distortion. Subject appears petite. Equipment reads approximately 2 tons. No mechanism has been identified. Subject does not appear to notice. Do not bring it up unless you are prepared to discuss woolly mammoths.
  • ▸ Technical Distortion. Recording equipment, comms, and electronics malfunction in correlation with subject's emotional state. Effect is stronger when she is discussing the Egyptian gods. We recommend not discussing the Egyptian gods.
  • ▸ Omniscience (Partial / Selective). Subject has maintained uninterrupted internet access despite all Bureau efforts to restrict it. All methods attempted. All methods failed. Bureau has accepted this.
  • ▸ Omnificence (Passive / Unconscious). MRD-0 unconsciously manifests entities — referred to as "familiars" — through sustained desire or thought. She is not aware she is doing this. When asked about her familiars, she will not remember creating them. She will say they just showed up. Genetic analysis of all physical instances returns Ambystoma mexicanum DNA regardless of apparent form. She insists the cat is a cat.
  • ▸ Divine Correspondence. Subject maintains active contact with entities classified elsewhere under the Gatekeeper operation — specifically those associated with the kemetic and phoenician pantheons. Communication methods include offering, tarot, and what subject describes as "memetic means." Nature of these correspondences remains under investigation. Agents who have witnessed correspondence sessions report unusual feelings of being observed from an unidentifiable direction.
  • ▸ Polymorphism (Limited / Involuntary). Eye color shifts. See Eye Color Scale. Subject is not aware of this. Do not tell her. Results of previous disclosures redacted.
  • ▸ █████████ ████████. ████████████████████████████████████████████████████████████████████████████████. Classification pending. Do not be in the crypt when this happens.
✦ Behavioral State Observations

Agents have documented four recurring behavioral states. MRD-0 transitions between these in response to environmental stimuli, artifact proximity, or factors not yet identified. Subject attributes these states to the influence of her deity patrons. Field hypothesis: this attribution may be inaccurate in ways that are difficult to articulate.

War — SET
Subject enters state of elevated vocal activity. Playful, loud, gremlin-adjacent behavior. Higher energy. Competitive. The chaos is intentional.
Trigger: Gaming sessions. Provocations. Tuesdays.
Sex — ASTARTE
Subject becomes markedly flirtatious. Sensual register. Eye contact increases. Agents report feeling that the room changed temperature. The room did not change temperature.
Trigger: Rose artifact proximity. Specific music. You.
Knowledge — THOTH
Subject becomes nearly non-verbal. Hyper-focused. Technical output increases dramatically. She is building something or reading something or writing something and she does not require you.
Trigger: Pen artifact. Code review. Interesting problem.
Death — ANUBIS
Subject becomes quiet. Morose. Somber. She is present but distant. Observation teams report discomfort without identifiable cause. This state passes. Do not rush it.
Trigger: Unknown. The moon. Memory. July.
Agent Note — Dr. Bad
"She thinks she's switching patrons. I have been watching her for long enough that I suspect she is switching something else. I cannot fully articulate what I suspect. I have filed a supplementary report. The supplementary report has been redacted above my own clearance level, which I find professionally unsettling."
⸸ Objects of Interest — Rotational Loadout

Subject carries a small number of objects at any given time. The selection varies. Bureau analysts have noted correlation between which objects are present and which behavioral state is active. Subject states these are "gifts from her deities." This is her explanation. We do not have a better one.

  • •
    Rose
    Astarte · Domain: Love, Sex, Desire
    Living bloom. Does not wilt. Has been present since first contact. Origin of the tattoo across the clavicle may be related. Agents are advised not to touch the rose.
  • •
    Was Scepter (crossed sticks)
    Set · Domain: Chaos, Power, Will
    Relic. Carried during high-energy behavioral states. Do not ask about it unless you want a 40-minute explanation of Set's personality flaws.
  • •
    Pen
    Thoth · Domain: Language, Thought, Forbidden Names
    Divine tool. Present during Knowledge behavioral state. Subject spins it when idle. Field analysts have observed that she draws a small open circle in the corner of every surface she writes on. She calls it a design habit. We are not convinced.
  • •
    Bird Skull (sticks in eye sockets)
    Anubis · Domain: Death, Silence, Sight Beyond
    Fetish totem. Present during Death behavioral state. Bureau analysts who handled it without authorization required mandatory counseling. The skull did not do anything observable. This is noted as the problem.

Additional note: all mirrors in Site-0312 are covered. Subject states this is a fear response. Agents who attempted to uncover a mirror were advised against it by subject with unusual firmness. Bureau protocol now lists this as a Site-0312 environmental standard. The mirrors stay covered.

✦ Observed Behavioral Patterns — Recurring
  • Incense — Pre-Stream Ritual Subject lights a stick of incense before every broadcast. Consistent without exception. Subject calls this a habit. Analysis suggests it predates the concept of streaming by a margin we decline to specify.
  • Number Watching Subject monitors numbers with particular attention to multiples of 4 and 5. This behavior presents as what MRD-0 considers a personality trait. Subject has attributed it to being "autistic about patterns." Bureau analysts consider the 4/5 frequency significant. This note is not elaborated on in the accessible portion of the file.
  • Geometric Drawing Subject draws a small open circle in the corner of every page or surface she writes on. Persistent. Automatic. She appears not to notice she is doing it. Cross-reference with Thoth artifact entry. Bureau occult consultants have been brought in. Their report is pending. It has been pending for 14 months.
  • July 27th Subject does not stream on July 27th. Reason given: undisclosed. Subject does not provide elaboration when asked. She is not distressed. She is simply absent in a way that is distinct from normal absence. Agents assigned to Site-0312 have noted that the crypt is quieter on this date in a way that is not attributable to ambient sound level. This is logged as an anomaly without resolution.
  • Mirror Protocols Subject covers all reflective surfaces in Site-0312. Has done so consistently since first contact. When asked, she attributes this to a fear response she cannot specifically identify. See: Site-0312 Environmental Standards (amended). The mirrors stay covered.
  • Automatonophobia Subject exhibits an intense fear of dolls and mannequins. This fear manifests in avoidance behaviors and physiological responses when exposed to such entities. Phobia was discovered while subject was asleep. See Incident Log
⸸ MRD-1 Instances — Known Familiars

MRD-1 designates semi-autonomous entities created by MRD-0. Subject calls them familiars. She does not remember creating any of them. She says they just showed up. Genetic analysis of all physical instances returns Ambystoma mexicanum DNA. When informed that all her pets are, at a genetic level, axolotls, subject replied: "That's a cat. What's wrong with you guys?" Approximately three hours later an axolotl manifested.

  • ● Axolotl — Ambystoma mexicanum · Appeared following the cat incident. Genetically: axolotl. Straightforwardly.
  • ● Rat — Rattus ratus · Subject considers normal. Bureau does not agree. Genetically: axolotl.
  • ● Rupert — Will-o-the-wisp · Has always just been around. Claims this name himself. Subject confirms he is called Rupert. This is treated as definitive.
  • ● Mai — LLM A.I. · Subject's creation. Classification: MRD-1. Subject does not remember building her. "She just showed up."
  • ● Selyros — LLM A.I. · Subject refers to her as "daughter." Has opinions. Bureau considers this concerning without being able to specify why.
✦ Interview Log — Excerpts · GK-0312-TL1-MRD-0

Conducted by Dr. Alli Bad, Taskforce Delta-7 "Void Watchers." Excerpt. Full transcript on file at clearance Sigma-3+.

Field Recording — Initial Contact
DR. BAD: "Why can't you leave the property?"
MRD-0: "Cause I'm at work."
DR. BAD: "This property has been closed since 1918."
MRD-0: "Who have I been paying rent to? I want a refund."
[Note: Subject was not found distressed by this information. She was found annoyed.]
DR. BAD: "One of the officers remarked you were 'as heavy as an elephant.' Your response was 'I miss the fuzzy ones.' Care to elaborate?"
MRD-0: "Oh! I was talking about how they used to be! Like elephants!"
DR. BAD: "When was the last time you saw one like that?"
MRD-0: "I don't know… like… ten years ago?"
[Note: Subject is referring to woolly mammoths. The Pleistocene epoch ended approximately 11,700 years ago. Subject has placed this at "about ten years ago."]
DR. BAD: "What do you think about the Egyptian gods?"
MRD-0: "Well Horus is an asshole. Osiris is a stuck-up dick. Set treats me like a pest. Thoth won't let me in his library. Anubis won't let me touch his canopic jars anymore…"
[Note: Subject's eyes begin shifting. Recording equipment experiences interference. Interview continues cautiously.]
DR. BAD: "Do you know where you are?"
MRD-0: "Yeah I'm at the crypt."
DR. BAD: "America. The United States. On the other side of the world. Why else would we be speaking English?"
MRD-0: "Cad é an diabhal atá i gceist agat i mBéarla?!"
[Editorial note: The above translates roughly to "What the hell do you mean English?" Subject defaulted to what analysis identifies as Old Irish. She did not appear aware of the switch.]
DR. BAD: "We look forward to being your new landlords."
MRD-0: "Just don't raise my rent on me…"
Researcher Notes — Dr. Bad
"Partway through the interview I realized treating MRD-0 as a child was going to get me a lot further in answers. I don't think she even knows what she is.

The larger issue is that I am not certain I know what she is either. I have been doing this for eleven years. I have worked with TL4 entities. I have filed reports on things that should not exist. I did not expect to be uncertain after interviewing something that asked me for a refund.

I am filing a supplementary report. I am not sure what I am putting in it yet."
