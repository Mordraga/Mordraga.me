Devil's Advocate

Rules of the Debate

✦ The Golden Rule

You are here to defend the side you are given. Your actual opinion does not matter. The Cauldron chooses your position, and once chosen, you commit to the bit.

✦ Etiquette

Argue the position, not the person. Be ridiculous. Be theatrical. Bring receipts. Challenge arguments freely, but don't turn a stupid debate into an actual fight.

Above all else Take the argument seriously.
Do not take yourselves seriously.

✦ Rules of the Debate

1

No Choosing Sides

Your side is assigned at random. You may agree with it, hate it, or consider it an affront to everything you believe. Defend it anyway.

2

Fifteen Minutes to Conspire

Once sides are revealed, contestants mute in Discord and return to their own chats. You have 15 minutes to research, gather arguments, consult your chat, and prepare your case.

3

Make Your Case

Return to The Debate Hall when called. Each contestant gets up to 15 minutes to argue their position.

4

New Evidence May Be Answered

If a contestant introduces a new argument or piece of evidence, the other side gets to respond. The debate continues until both sides have made their case.

5

Chat Judges Persuasion

Chat votes before and after the debate. Victory goes to whoever moved the audience furthest toward their assigned side. You are trying to change minds.
