Getting Ready

✦ Before the Ritual

Before your round begins, take a moment to get your stream, audio, and show tools ready. Once everything is set, all that's left is to wait for the Cauldron.

✦ Pre-Show Checklist

1

Prepare Your Stream

Make sure your mic, model, capture, and Discord audio are working before your round begins.

2

Join the Debate Hall

Join the Discord call before your scheduled round and wait for the hosts to get everyone situated.

3

Open Your Contestant Link

Open the contestant link sent to you by the hosts. Your link belongs to your seat for that round. If your seat is reset, you'll receive a new contestant link.

✦ OBS Overlay

Add the show overlay to OBS as a Browser Source. The show uses the same session between rounds, so once you've added it, you can leave it configured.
